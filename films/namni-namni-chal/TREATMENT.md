# Namni Namni Chal: treatment
Music video for the owner's Gujarati romantic song (94.08 s, 74 BPM, from `audio/song.mp3`), in a new style, **Pichhwai Night** (`style/STYLE.md`).
1920×1080, 24 fps. Gujarati subtitles with English translation. See `LYRICS.md` for the analysis that drives every choice.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The unspoken courtship**: he watches her from afar through the verses (eyes, idol, tulsi), takes one shy step on the bridge, and the chorus becomes her walk through the festive village; in the last chorus they dance together under the full moon. Follows the song's arc exactly.
2. **One day at the tulsi**: dawn puja, noon at the well, evening garba, night: one place, four lights. Strong on the tulsi image but loses the chorus's walk.
3. **The pichhwai as a book**: each line a new painted panel, flat tableaux. Faithful to the medium but static; a 74 BPM song needs movement in the chorus.
**Chosen: 1.** It is literally what the lyrics describe: private gaze → sacred images → conditional confession → open praise of her walk.

## Logline and arc
A young man who has never spoken to her watches a young woman at her tulsi, and on the night of the fair finally steps into her circle.
Setup (0–34 s) gaze and devotion · turn (48–58 s) his step, the music's break · ending (73–94 s) together in the moonlight, the cloth rolls closed.
