# Under the Stars: treatment

Lyric music video, 80s Cel Anime. Instrumental (no sung vocal): the lead synth-brass/sax line *is* the melody of the lyric, and each line appears on the tape as a sung-along title.
56 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · 120 BPM, 4/4 (1 bar = 2.0 s, 28 bars) · key D major, last chorus lifts to Eb.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The rooftop leap**: a girl alone on a rooftop under the stars; on the drop she runs and jumps off, the city falls away and she flies; dawn breaks. Clear one-goal/one-turn arc that literally plays "learn to fly" and "into the light".
2. **Four windows**: four people in four lit windows of one tower, each line of lyric visits one; at the end all four lights go out and they meet on the roof. Richer, but four characters = four heads to draw well; weaker turn.
3. **Cassette mixtape**: a walkman's tape plays the song; each line is a memory seen through the tape window. "A song as an object"; nice, but the lyrics are about the future, not the past.

**Chosen: 1, the rooftop leap.** It is the only one where the turn (the leap) is the chorus itself, and the ending can repaint the opening composition at dawn ("an earlier composition repainted": what changed).

## Logline and arc
A girl on a city rooftop at night stops watching the stars and goes to them.
- Setup (bars 1–12): night city, alone, small flat hero in a huge painted world; she looks up.
- Turn (bar 12→13): one beat of real silence, extreme close-up on her eyes, impact frame, she leaps.
- Ending (bars 21–28): flight over the city into first light; the opening rooftop repainted at dawn, her silhouette rimmed in gold.

## Benchmark
Late-80s OVA opening sequences in general (city-pop openings: big chrome title, a run, a flight over a neon city at night).
- Learn: cutting on bars, held drawings with moving light, backlit title, speed grammar on the chorus.
- Don't take: any character, mecha, logo, shot or melody.

## Cue map
120 BPM, 16 steps/bar. Sections: intro (1–4) · verse (5–12) · drop (13–14) · chorus A (15–20) · chorus B / lift (21–24) · outro (25–28).
- Intro: LinnDrum-style kick/hat, gated snare on 2&4 from bar 3, arpeggiated analog synth in 16ths, string pad, FM bell sparkle on bar 1.
- Verse: + slap bass, FM e-piano maj7/9 chords, lead (synth-brass, soft) plays the line-1/2 melody.
- Bar 12: drum fill, beat 4 silent.
- Drop/chorus: + chorus guitar 16th chanks, big toms, lead louder (sax-like FM), lines 3/4 melody.
- Lift: everything up a semitone at bar 21.
- Outro: band drops to pad + e-piano + arp; final brass hit + bell on title; tape stop (pitch-down) at 55.5.
Progression (D): | Gmaj7 | A6 | F#m7 | Bm9 | (verse) and | Gmaj7 | A | Bm7 | D/F# | Em9 | A | D | D | (chorus), transposed +1 at bar 21.

## Titles and lyric type
Lyrics as tape subtitles: Barlow Semi Condensed 600, cream-yellow, thick dark outline, ~96 px from bottom, revealed word by word on the melody notes (karaoke feel, words already-sung brighter). End title: heavy italic chrome "UNDER THE STARS", glowing via the emissive layer, 12 fps flicker on, star glint.
No voice → no TTS; `.srt` exported with the four lines.

## Changes made during production
- Shot 1 is a **pull-out from one star** (zoom from 5.2× to 1×) while the rooftop slides up into frame, not a crane down: the demo opens on a crane.
- Sung vocal: none (the owner chose an instrumental + lyric video). The lead synth plays one note per lyric syllable, and the on-screen words light up on those same notes (karaoke), both read from `song.json`, which `music/score.py` writes.
- Mastering in `mix.py` (BS.1770 loudness + 4× true-peak limiter) instead of `mux.sh`: this machine's ffmpeg is 6.x.
